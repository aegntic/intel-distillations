# Faceless Channel Niches

The 61-niche catalog from the Steffens teardown, deduplicated against the harvest brief's niche material, plus the ranked fit for an agent-studio operator with a domain portfolio. All channel stats are creator-shown via NextLev/VidIQ and carry [unverified]. Related: [content-and-seo-ops](content-and-seo-ops.md), [voice-and-brand-laws](voice-and-brand-laws.md), [validation-tactics](validation-tactics.md).

## Synthesis

Stated selection criteria: small/new channels with outsized views (supply/demand gap), consistent packaging, simple production (AI script + voiceover + images), decent RPM, and short videos signaling "the niche is still quite empty" [NAB9PyN2-3E 1:22, 23:16, 27:24, 54:34]. The niche-transfer method multiplies winners: take a proven format to an adjacent space (other sports, US weapons to other countries, "12 Italian home ideas" elsewhere, senior repackaging) [NAB9PyN2-3E 12:22, 19:43, 32:34, 1:05:37].

Two opposite operating profiles: news-style niches (WNBA, royals, politics) need daily posting and accept ban risk; documentary/history niches (hiking, tech history, forgotten weapons, boats, planes) run longer with fewer uploads [NAB9PyN2-3E 8:20, 24:11, 1:07:23]. Monetization mechanics: RPM math (views/1000 x RPM) plus digital-product upsells on top (27/47/97 tiers shown on one channel); double down on outliers "until it does not work anymore" [NAB9PyN2-3E 28:19, 28:48].

Risk tiers flagged honestly by the creator himself: politics ("you have to be prepared to get banned"), voice-clone channels ("you will 100% get demonetized... if you're lucky you can at least get one payout"; use a second AdSense), military content (isolate AdSense), and compilations must add voiceover or opinion to survive monetization review [NAB9PyN2-3E 21:29, 52:18, 1:17:58, 4:07]. AI avatars are monetization-safe for general content but not legal/finance/health advice [NAB9PyN2-3E 10:36]. Persistence rule: never judge a channel before ~5-15 videos; multiple channels had first videos of 40-750 views before 300k+ outliers [NAB9PyN2-3E 47:32, 56:10].

Representative proof points (all [unverified], creator-shown): Tech District (tech-company rise-and-fall docs; 42 videos, 1.8M views in first month, $6 RPM, ~= $12,500/month-one claim) [57:21]; sleep/ambient at $30-40 RPM with 80-85k views/week [1:12:48]; True Respect (AI image transformations; $26 RPM, one video called $10,000, "crush this now then you're out") [1:16:02]; The Weatherman Asia ($3 RPM but $6,700 in 30 days on volume) [1:03:31]; Home Hazard (47 videos, 700k views, plus $27/$47/$97 owned product) [27:50]; jewelry/valuables "is your old X worth money" format breaking out across object classes [11:53].

## Key claims

- Short videos in a niche signal the niche is empty; as entrants arrive, video length grows [NAB9PyN2-3E 27:24].
- Double down on outliers "until it does not work anymore" [NAB9PyN2-3E 28:48].
- Ban-risk tiers: politics (expect bans), voice-clone (100% eventual demonetization, second AdSense), military (isolate AdSense); compilations need added voiceover or opinion [NAB9PyN2-3E 21:29, 52:18, 1:17:58, 4:07].
- AI avatars are monetization-safe except for legal/finance/health advice [NAB9PyN2-3E 10:36].
- Persistence: never judge a channel before ~5-15 videos; first videos of 40-750 views preceded 300k+ outliers [NAB9PyN2-3E 47:32, 56:10].

## Ranked fit for an agent studio with a domain portfolio

1. Tech-company rise-and-fall documentaries (~$6 RPM, long videos): research-heavy, evergreen, agent pipeline native, low ban risk.
2. "Is your old X worth money" valuables: tiny content cost, affiliate/estate-auction layer, domain-sliceable per object class; keep valuation claims sourced.
3. Country/geography transfer of proven formats (weather news, home ideas, China tech, forgotten weapons per country): one validated format, N domains.
4. Documentary evergreen cluster (planes, fleet, weapons, hiking, Amish, first humans): stable views, low cadence, quality-gated pipeline friendly.
5. Sleep/ambient at $30-40 RPM: highest RPM in the list, generation-heavy, verification gates beat the slideshow competition.
6. AI-avatar expert channels with digital-product attach; avoid regulated topics.
7. Informational channel plus owned digital product (Home Hazard pattern): best monetization per view.
8. AI-transformation one-offs at $26 RPM: time-boxed batch, one domain, expect algorithm risk.
9. Celebrity nostalgia compilation at volume: cadence feasible with agents, rights review is the risk.
10. AI-generated footage experiments: first-mover space where agents are the differentiator.

Explicitly avoided: politics, royal-family news, voice-clone, and military tiers; ban warnings plus AdSense contamination are wrong for a portfolio whose value is the domains and standing channels.

## Contradictions

- Steffens tells beginners to find evergreen niches with consistent packaging, yet much of his own list is trend-chasing news with daily cadence and ban risk; opposite operational profiles for a solo operator [source-six-deep, contradictions].
- The niche video multiplies views x RPM as "earnings" while the Instagram video counts an Amazon product's gross revenue as influencer income; both overstate take-homes to sell courses. Mechanics real, numbers marketing [source-six-deep, contradictions].

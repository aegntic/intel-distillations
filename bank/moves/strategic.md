# Strategic theses

Bigger-picture dollar theses from the corpus, each with a falsifier and a
review date. These are hypotheses with tests, not projections; creator
revenue figures stay attributed and [unverified].

## 1. Offer ladder: SOP wedge, then KB-agent anchor [mv-004]

The wiki's anchor sits at 10-25k setup plus 1-2k/month retainer
[GkHwnQdoDpM 35:43]. Two comparisons now bracket it. Kirk sells an internal
KB agent at $20-40k priced against the replaced employee's salary
[unverified] [f24 15:26]. Pigford runs flat $1,000/month done-for-you SEO
[unverified], and his ARR figures ($570K, then $474K in three weeks) are
doubted by the very hosts repeating them [f56 00:47, 2026-11-18]. Thesis:
the SOP-to-skills flagship holds its rung, a KB-agent offer prices one rung
higher on identical delivery machinery, and deposits settle it. Falsifier:
10 qualified calls, zero deposits at the anchor. Review: after the first 5
booked calls.

## 2. The harness is the demo: software-factory delivery [mv-007]

Grill intent, split releases into issues, agents run
plan-implement-validate, one batch review in staging [f02 11:57, 10:24,
2026-10-27]. Thesis: the studio's delivery loop itself becomes the sales
artifact for agent-development clients, the same way intel-distillations is
the artifact for the distillation pitch. Falsifier: three consecutive forge
releases need more rework under batch review than interactive sessions.

## 3. Proactive agents as a product category, proven by one public demo [mv-005, mv-018]

The proactive formula is durable agent plus trigger inside a sandbox
[f13 3:04, 2026-10-23]. One public demo makes it sellable. Two candidate
patterns exist now: the hype/fact/signal curator [mv-005] and the weekly
money-finder, which surfaced 20-year-old unclaimed property plus a $1,000
bank bonus for one viewer [f57 08:48, 09:06, 2026-11-18; mv-018]. Run one
at a time. Falsifier: 30 days of upkeep over ~15 min/week, or zero agents
counted as doing useful work.

## 4. Defended channels beat slop at the differentiation game [mv-011]

Three sources triangulate the same read: unmodified output reads as slop,
own face and voice defend channel momentum, avatar-only content is "the
slop category" [f05 53:35, 57:03; f06 56:04, 2026-10-27]. Ownership adds a
second rule: best-of channels in underserved verticals beat selling
clipping as a service, because the audience compounds while the deliverable
does not [f56 15:15, 2026-11-18]. Falsifier: a 15-video A/B where
face-and-voice underperforms on watch time, shares, saves.

## 5. Feature-count discipline as margin law [mv-012]

Each added feature multiplies vibe-coded app maintenance roughly 10x
[f06 1:36:16, 2026-10-27]. One core conversion loop, no second feature
until the first retains payers. Falsifier: any second feature shipping
before the first has paying-user evidence.

## 6. The audit-magnet funnel as the studio's front door [mv-015]

Three independent sources run the same funnel shape. Give the diagnosis
away at near-zero marginal cost, about $5 of API spend against a $2,000
human equivalent; charge a small refund-backed tier; sell the fix sprint
behind it [f17 07:34, 25:38, 27:07; f29 free-sample-first, 2026-11-18].
Thesis: this funnel, not content marketing, is the cheapest qualified-lead
engine for the SOP-to-skills ladder, because the audit artifact doubles as
the demo. Falsifier: first 10 delivered audits yield zero paid follow-ons
within 30 days. Review: after the first 10 audits.

## 7. Vertical local-SEO as the quarter-scale services bet [mv-017]

The 7-step system is fully specified in-source: grid scans, 30-50 location
pages per metro, 3-6 pages per month with verified indexing, results read
on 2-4 week windows [f54 03:38, 10:21, 12:01, 33:15, 2026-11-18]. Agent
generation changes the unit economics that kept this a manual-agency
service, and the vertical filter (who cannot DIY this) picks the trade
[f56 02:44, 10:34, 2026-11-18]. Thesis: one trade, one metro, $2-5K/month
packaging reproduces the agency outcome at a solo-studio cost structure.
Falsifier: at 90 days, under half the pages indexed or zero booked jobs
attributable. Review: 90 days after pilot start.

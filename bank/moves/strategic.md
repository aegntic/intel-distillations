# Strategic theses

Bigger-picture dollar theses from the corpus, each with a falsifier and a
review date. These are hypotheses with tests, not projections; creator
revenue figures stay attributed and [unverified].

## 1. Offer ladder: SOP wedge, then KB-agent anchor [mv-004]

The wiki's anchor (10-25k setup plus 1-2k/month retainer [GkHwnQdoDpM 35:43])
now has two independent comparisons: Kirk's internal KB agent at $20-40k
priced against the replaced employee's salary [unverified] [f24 15:26], and
Pigford's flat $1,000/month done-for-you SEO at ~30 clients claiming $570K
ARR in month one [unverified] [f17 0:00]. Thesis: the SOP-to-skills flagship
holds the 10-25k rung, and a KB-agent offer (internal knowledge base, branded
dashboard, WhatsApp keyword trigger) prices one rung higher on identical
delivery machinery. Falsifier: 10 qualified calls, zero deposits at the
anchor. Review: after the first 5 booked calls.

## 2. The harness is the demo: software-factory delivery [mv-007]

Grill intent, split releases into issues, agents run plan-implement-validate,
one batch review in staging [f02 11:57, 10:24, 2026-10-27]. Thesis: the
studio's own delivery loop becomes the sales artifact for agent-development
clients, the same way intel-distillations is the artifact for the
distillation pitch. Falsifier: three consecutive forge releases need more
rework under batch review than interactive sessions.

## 3. Proactive agents as a product category, proven by one public curator [mv-005]

The proactive formula (durable agent plus trigger inside a sandbox [f13 3:04,
2026-10-23]) is one public demo away from sellable: a hype/fact/signal
curator running out-of-loop for pennies per sweep. Falsifier: 30 days of
upkeep over ~15 min/week, or zero agents counted as doing useful work.

## 4. Defended channels beat slop at the differentiation game [mv-011]

Three sources triangulate: unmodified output reads as slop, own face and
voice defend channel momentum, avatar-only content is "the slop category"
[f05 53:35, 57:03; f06 56:04, 2026-10-27]. Thesis: real-face packaging on
high-velocity channels outperforms anonymous equivalents and survives
platform slop flags. Falsifier: 15-video A/B where face-and-voice
underperforms on watch time, shares, saves.

## 5. Feature-count discipline as margin law [mv-012]

Each added feature multiplies vibe-coded app maintenance roughly 10x
[f06 1:36:16, 2026-10-27]. Thesis: studio-built apps ship one core
conversion loop and defer feature two until paying-user evidence exists.
Falsifier: any second feature shipping before feature one retains payers.
